## What It Is

MiroFish is a swarm intelligence engine that uses multi-agent systems to predict the future. The core idea is building a "parallel digital world" with thousands of agents, each having its own personality, memory, and behavioral logic, then letting them interact freely to simulate social dynamics.

Unlike a static LLM that answers from fixed knowledge — MiroFish generates emergent behavior from large-scale agent interactions.

## Core Capabilities

- Accepts seed materials in natural language
- Automatically builds a simulated world with personas and a relationship graph
- Allows variable injection mid-simulation for counterfactual scenario testing
- Produces prediction reports with deep interaction via ReportAgent

## Use Cases

- **Policy & PR**: Test public reaction before an announcement
- **Finance**: Simulate market sentiment from news signals
- **Entertainment**: Predict or extend a novel, simulate fictional worlds
- **Research**: Study social emergence and collective behavior

## Technical Foundation

Powered by the [[OASIS]] framework from [[CAMEL-AI]].
Uses [[Zep Cloud]] for per-agent long-term memory.
Uses [[GraphRAG]] to build knowledge structure from seed materials.
