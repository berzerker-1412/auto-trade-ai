AI research organization that maintains the [[ai-research-skills|AI Research Skills Library]] — an open-source library of 87 skills enabling AI agents to autonomously conduct end-to-end ML research.

Also runs `orchestra-research.com` where all 87 skills are synced and can be added to projects with one click.

Published the npm package `@orchestra-research/ai-research-skills` for one-command installation across all major coding agents (Claude Code, Gemini CLI, Cursor, OpenCode, Hermes Agent).
