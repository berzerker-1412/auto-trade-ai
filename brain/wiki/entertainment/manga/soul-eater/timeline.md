## Ancient Era

- **Distant past** — Eibon, the great sorcerer, writes blueprints for Demon Weapons inspired by Excalibur. The Nakatsukasa Clan becomes one of the first Demon Weapon families.
- **~800 years before main story** — Witch **Arachne Gorgon** uses Eibon's plans to create the first Demon Weapon, splitting the soul-weapon bond away from its original design. Death pursues her to Loew Village; she escapes by planting her soul into the Oldest Golem and scattering her body as spiders across the world — entering 800 years of dormancy.
- **Ancient era** — Death seals the first Kishin, **Asura**, beneath what will become DWMA, wrapping him in his own skin and burying him under Death City.
- **Founding** — Lord Death establishes the Death Weapon Meister Academy (DWMA) in Death City. Because he anchored himself to the land to imprison Asura, he can never leave Death City.

## Pre-Story (~1 year before main)

Soul Eater NOT! takes place here — Tsugumi Harudori arrives at DWMA as a new student. Events establish factions, background characters, and the day-to-day world before the main plot begins.

## Key Date

**April 1st** — Anniversary of DWMA's founding. Also the day Asura was resurrected, and the day someone under alias "M" borrowed the Book of Eibon manuscript. Recurring symbolic date throughout the manga.
