Manga by Atsushi Ohkubo, serialized in Monthly Shōnen Magazine 2004–2013 (113 chapters). Anime adaptation by Bones in 2008 (51 episodes) — diverges from manga around episode 36. Prequel spin-off: **Soul Eater NOT!** (2011–2014).

**Setting:** Death City, Nevada — home of the Death Weapon Meister Academy (DWMA), run by Lord Death. Students pair as Meisters + Demon Weapons, hunting evil souls to prevent a new Kishin from rising.
