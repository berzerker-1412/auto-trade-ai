## Main Trio (Meisters + Weapons)

### Maka Albarn + Soul "Eater" Evans

**Maka** — 2-star Meister, scythe specialist. Academically strongest of the main trio; highly disciplined. Possesses an **Anti-Demon Wavelength** — a unique innate ability that can purify and repel madness. Daughter of Spirit Albarn (Death Scythe) and a nameless weapon-meister mother.

**Soul Evans** — Demon Scythe. Transforms into a large scythe for Maka. Comes from a prestigious musical family; plays piano. After being cut by Ragnarok, Soul becomes infected with **Black Blood** — which he learns to control to access enormous power via Resonance with Maka. Has a **Grigori Soul** — a rare soul type that, fully awakened, grants flight.

---

### Black☆Star + Tsubaki Nakatsukasa

**Black☆Star** — Last survivor of the Star Clan, a clan of assassins eliminated by Death for going rogue. Raised at DWMA by Sid Barrett. Extremely loud and ego-driven — the antithesis of an assassin in personality, but catastrophically powerful in combat. Aims to surpass God. Uses a **Shadow Weapon Meister** style.

**Tsubaki Nakatsukasa** — Multi-form Demon Weapon, daughter of the Nakatsukasa Clan. Weapon forms: Smoke Bomb, Shuriken, Kusarigama, Ninja Sword, and **Uncanny Sword** (after absorbing her brother Masamune). The Uncanny Sword is the clan's legendary forbidden technique.

---

### Death the Kid + Liz & Patty Thompson

**Death the Kid** — Son of Lord Death, and a Shinigami in his own right. Partnered with twin pistols, Liz and Patty. Perfectionist to the point of dysfunction — obsessed with symmetry (his hair has three white Lines of Sanzu on the left, none on the right). As the Lines of Sanzu fully connect, his true Shinigami power awakens. Among the strongest characters by endgame.

**Elizabeth "Liz" Thompson** — Older of the twin sisters; transforms into a pistol (right hand). Pragmatic and street-smart; originally a gang leader with Patty on the streets of Brooklyn.

**Patricia "Patty" Thompson** — Younger twin; left-hand pistol. Appears childlike and cheerful — deceptively dangerous. Can solo kill Kishin-level enemies while laughing.

## Antagonists

### Medusa Gorgon
Witch and DWMA school nurse in disguise. The primary manipulator of the first half. Her goal: resurrect Asura to throw the world into madness, which she believes accelerates evolution. Uses Vector magic — arrows that control motion and matter. Possesses multiple bodies across the story. Mother of Crona.

### Crona
Medusa's child, raised in isolation as a weapon experiment. Fused with the Demon Sword **Ragnarok** — an embodiment of Black Blood. Deeply traumatized; oscillates between ally and enemy depending on Medusa's manipulation. By the end of the manga, Crona seals both themselves and Asura on the Moon.

### Asura (The Kishin)
The original Kishin — a shinigami who consumed souls out of fear, becoming the embodiment of **Madness**. Death sealed him under DWMA. After resurrection, his Madness Wavelength spreads globally, destabilizing everyone. Final boss of the series. Defeated by Maka's Anti-Demon Wavelength — specifically via courage and empathy rather than brute force.

### Arachne Gorgon
Medusa's elder sister. Creator of the first Demon Weapons 800 years ago; hid as a soul inside the Oldest Golem. Returns to lead **Arachnophobia**, a global organization opposing DWMA. Defeated during Operation Capture Baba Yaga Castle; her body taken over by Medusa.

### Noah
Collector obsessed with obtaining everything referenced in the Book of Eibon. Captures Kid. Multiple Noahs exist as personifications of the book's chapters (Greed, Wrath, etc.).
