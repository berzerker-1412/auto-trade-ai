A retrieval technique that builds a **knowledge graph** from documents and retrieves via graph traversal rather than pure vector similarity. Better at capturing relationships between entities.

[[MiroFish]] uses GraphRAG in the Graph Building step to construct relationship structures between agents from seed materials.
